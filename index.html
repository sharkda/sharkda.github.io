找車位Find Parking TW

臺北市與新北市的即時停車位數量，資料直接來自兩市政府的開放資料平臺。

這個 App 做什麼

  • 即時剩餘車位——不是我們數的，是市政府公布的。
  • 老實標示新鮮度。剛更新的數字是綠色，開始過時的會轉為灰色。數字的顏色代表新鮮度，大小代表剩的位子有多少。資料停更時我們不會假裝它還是新的。
  • 還沒到也能用。選一個目的地，地圖、搜尋與追蹤清單都會以該地運作，不必人在當地。
  • 追蹤清單。把常去的停車場釘起來，一眼看完。

目前只有臺北市與新北市

其他地區還沒有資料可以顯示。這是還沒做到，不是壞掉——我們依照提出的人數決定下一個開通的城市，所以告訴我們你需要哪裡是真的有用。

English

Find Parking TW (找車位) shows live parking-space counts for Taipei City and New Taipei City, straight from the two city governments' open-data platforms.

What it does

  • Live space counts — we do not count them ourselves; the city governments publish them.
  • Honest about freshness. A fresh count is green; an aging one fades to grey. Colour means freshness, size means how few spaces are left. When a city's feed goes quiet we say so rather than pretending the number is current.
  • Works before you arrive. Pick a destination and the whole app works from there — map, search and watch list — without being in the city.
  • A watch list for the car parks you use often.

Taipei City and New Taipei City only, for now

Outside those two the app has nothing to show yet — a gap, not a bug. We rank which city to add next by how many people ask for it, so telling us where you need it genuinely counts.

Need help?

Questions, wrong data, a city you want — it all lives on the support page. Also: privacy policy.
