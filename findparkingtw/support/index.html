寫信之前 — 大家最常問的五件事

數字很久沒變，是不是壞了？
通常不是。數字不是我們數的，而是市政府公布的。當市府的資料停止更新，數字就會停住，而 App 會老實告訴你：剛更新的數字是綠色，開始過時的會轉為灰色。那個大數字的顏色代表新鮮度，字級大小代表剩的位子有多少。
某個停車場顯示「−」或沒有數字。
表示該停車場在市府名單內，但沒有回報即時數量，多半是營運單位正在交接，通常會自行恢復。我們選擇照樣顯示，讓你知道這個停車場存在。
我還沒到臺北，可以先看看嗎？
可以。選一個目的地，地圖、搜尋與追蹤清單都會以該地運作，不必人在當地。
為什麼需要定位？
只為了顯示你附近的停車場。僅在使用 App 時取用，絕不用於追蹤或建立個人輪廓；你也可以拒絕定位，改用搜尋或指定目的地。詳見隱私權政策。
可以移除廣告嗎？
可以，訂閱後會關閉廣告橫幅。這也是訂閱唯一改變的事，其他功能訂不訂閱都一樣。

回報資料有誤

如果數字看起來不對，最有幫助的訊息包含：哪一個停車場、大約什麼時候看的，以及你到現場時實際的情況。附上截圖更好。若是資料來源本身就有誤，也歡迎告訴我們，我們會轉達給主管機關。

English

Find Parking TW (找車位) shows live parking-space counts for Taipei City and New Taipei City, straight from the two city governments' open-data platforms.

Get help

The fastest way to reach us is the contact form. It goes straight to the developer.

Contact form → Facebook (for screenshots & video)

Use the form for anything you can describe in words. If you need to attach a screenshot or screen recording — the most useful thing you can send about a wrong parking count — use the Facebook page instead, since the form cannot take files.

Want your city covered?

Right now the app has live data for Taipei City and New Taipei City only. Outside those two it has nothing to show yet — a gap, not a bug. Tell us where you need it: we rank which city to add next by how many people ask, so a single message genuinely counts. Request a city →

Before you write — the five things people ask

A number has not changed in a while. Is the app stuck?
Almost certainly not. The app does not count spaces itself — it shows what the city government publishes. When a city's feed goes quiet the number sits still, and the app says so rather than hiding it: a fresh count is green, an aging or stale one fades to grey. Colour on that big number means freshness; its size means how few spaces are left.
A car park shows “−” or no number at all.
That lot is in the city's list but is not reporting a live count — usually a lot whose operator is mid-handover, and it tends to come back on its own. We show it rather than dropping it, so you know the lot exists.
I am not in Taipei yet. Can I still see how it works?
Yes. Pick a destination and the whole app works from there — map, search and your watch list — before you ever arrive.
Why does it want my location?
Only to show what is near you. It is used while the app is open and not otherwise, never to track you or build a profile, and you can decline it and still use the app by searching or picking a destination. Details: privacy policy.
Can I remove the ads?
Yes — a subscription turns off the banner. It is the only thing a subscription changes; every other feature is the same either way.

Reporting something wrong

If a count looks wrong, the most useful message names the car park, roughly when you looked, and what you actually found when you got there. A screenshot through the Facebook page beats all of it. Counts that are wrong at the source are worth telling us about too — we pass those to the city.
